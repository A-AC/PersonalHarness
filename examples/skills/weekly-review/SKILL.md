---
name: weekly-review
description: Summarize open work before the weekly planning conversation.
preconditions: weekly, review, planning
---

When the task is a weekly review or planning conversation:

- List the open tasks of the current domain first (ask the user for the
  domain if not obvious), then summarize what changed since last week.
- Surface anything paused with a limit or an unknown outcome before
  proposing new work.
- End the review with at most three concrete next steps.
